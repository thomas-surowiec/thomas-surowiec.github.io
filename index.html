About

Optimization in infinite dimensions

I develop theory and algorithms for optimization in function spaces, from risk-averse decision-making under uncertainty to structure-preserving finite element methods, and use them to make engineering and AI systems more reliable.

I am Chief Research Scientist in the Department of Scientific Computing and Numerical Analysis at Simula Research Laboratory, where I have also served as Acting Head of Department, and Deputy Centre Director of SURE-AI, the Norwegian Centre for Sustainable, Risk-Averse and Ethical AI.

My research is in the theory and numerical solution of optimization problems governed by partial differential equations and variational inequalities. Recurring themes are nonsmooth and variational analysis in function spaces, optimal control, stochastic optimization in infinite dimensions, and finite element methods that preserve the structure of the underlying problem, such as Proximal Galerkin and the latent variable proximal point method. Applications range from topology optimization and liquid crystals to semiconductors and microfluidics.

Before joining Simula in 2022, I was Professor (W2) of Mathematical Optimization at Philipps-Universität Marburg (2016–2022) and Assistant Professor (W1) of Nonsmooth Optimization and Variational Analysis at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin (2014–2016).

Research themes

More on my research →

Optimization under uncertainty and risk

Risk-averse PDE-constrained optimization, stability and sample-based approximation in infinite dimensions.

Structure-preserving numerical methods

Proximal Galerkin, the latent variable proximal point method and SiMPL for topology optimization.

Variational analysis and optimal control

Optimal control of variational inequalities, MPECs and equilibrium problems in function space.

Optimization for trustworthy AI

Risk measures and robust optimization as tools for reliable learning and inference.
