Optimization in Oslo

OiO seminar archive

A research seminar featuring international speakers in optimization under uncertainty, PDE-constrained optimization, optimal control of complex systems, uncertainty quantification and scientific machine learning, with applications from imaging to materials science. Talks were 50 minutes plus discussion, open to students, postdocs and everyone interested in continuous optimization in Oslo.

The seminar is currently on hiatus. Recordings of past talks are on the OiO YouTube channel; click a title for the speaker’s abstract and bio.
